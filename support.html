Cozely

Support

Cozely is a date‑night game you play together on two phones.

How do we start?

One partner taps Start a new couple and shares the code. The other taps Join with a code and enters it. Pick a game and play. Prefer one phone? Several games have a "Play on one phone" mode.

What's free, and what's Premium?

The core experience includes couples games, daily questions, and your private shared space. Cozely Premium unlocks premium games, guided nights, memories, and shared-space styles for both partners. Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly unlock the same Premium benefits. Current prices and any introductory offer are shown in the app before purchase.

How does approximate distance work?

Both partners opt in separately under You Two → Distance. Cozely stores only each partner's latest coarse location, not a location history. Background refresh is optional; either person can pause sharing and clear their saved location at any time.

How do I manage my subscription?

Cozely Premium renews through your Apple Account. Manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

How do I delete my data?

Remove any photo or memory in the app, or fully erase your account in Settings → Delete account. Deletion is permanent for both partners.

Still need help?

For non-private product help, open a request in the Cozely support tracker. Do not include account credentials, private couple content, or other personal information.
